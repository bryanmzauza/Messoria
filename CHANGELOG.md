# Changelog

What changes from one release of Messoria to the next. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-06

The first release.

### Added

- Play alone or with friends, in a world hosted from the game or on a dedicated server, with movement that stays smooth over a slow connection.
- First-person and third-person views.
- Smooth ground you can dig and raise with the shovel, the same for every player.
- Days of 20 minutes, a year of four seasons, and day and night.
- The day ends when players sleep: everyone in a hosted world, a share of them on a dedicated server; whoever is still up at 02:00 collapses and wakes up at home.
- Energy, spent by work and restored by sleep.
- A hotbar and a backpack; food that spoils turns into compost.
- Farming: till with the hoe, plant, water or let the rain do it, and harvest five crops in their seasons, better with fertilizer.
- A village market whose prices fall as everyone sells the same thing and recover day by day, with a daily limit to what each player sells.
- Money of your own, and money given to other players.
- Worlds saved every few minutes, at dawn and on leaving, and servers that know you when you come back.
- Trees, bushes, rocks, flowers and mushrooms across the valley, changing color with the seasons, and snow in winter.
- A sky with a sun, a moon, clouds and stars.
- Sprinting, and bumping into trees, rocks and walls.
- Sounds and particles for work and trade, and a word on screen when an action does nothing and why.
- What the crosshair is on is named under it, with how long a crop has left and whether it needs water.
- A game menu with options for volume, mouse sensitivity, field of view and graphics quality, kept between sessions.
- The axe fells trees for wood and the pickaxe breaks rocks for stone; stumps grow back into trees, and berry bushes give berries again after a few days.
- A cabin of your own, built from a deed, with a bed to sleep in and a chest that keeps your things.
- Animated characters that hold their tools, crops drawn at every stage of growth, and item icons.
- Light and color that follow the hour and the weather, with soft shadows and haze at dawn and dusk.
- Models and textures drawn for the game, in one pixel-art style, swaying in the wind.
- A valley about two kilometers across, with hills, woods, meadows, a river and roads, grown from the world's seed and drawn out to the horizon.
- Fenced farm lots along the roads, claimed at the village notice board: the first is free, more can be bought, and owners invite who may work them.
- A village of streets and houses around a square, with a tavern, a tailor, a barber and a blacksmith to walk into.
- Ten villagers with days of their own: they keep shop, sit on benches, chat, tend their gardens, eat at the tavern and go home at night.
- Talk to the villagers and give them gifts; friendship grows in hearts, and brings warmer words, presents and a friend's price at their shop.
- A list of the villagers on `V`, with your friendship with each and where they are.
- A character creator on first arrival: body, skin, eyes, hair, an outfit and a name.
- Clothes and hats from the tailor, worn from the wardrobe in your cabin, and new hair from the barber.
- Music for each season, and the sound of wind, birds, crickets, rain and the river, muffled indoors; music has a volume of its own.
- A title screen: continue, start a new world, see and delete your worlds, host for friends, and join by address with the addresses joined lately.
- From the game menu, open or close the world to friends and see who is here; players turned away are told why.
- A card of controls on first arrival, and on `F1` after.
- Players' names over their heads and wherever players are listed.
